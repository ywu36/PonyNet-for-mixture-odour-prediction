O2 and O2-VAE: where the mixture is merged

Both are open-POM (graph network) rebuilt in PyTorch and trained on single molecules (GS-LF). A mixture runs every molecule through the same encoder, merges the vectors at one layer, and the layers after the merge (the decoder) turn the merged vector into 138 odour words.

O2's decoder turns every large mixture into nearly the same long, confident profile. The VAE's merged point moves toward the centre of its latent space (|z| 3.8 → 1.2) and decodes to a shorter, whiter profile, closer to the molecules but still whiter than a plain mean.
